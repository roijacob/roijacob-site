Hi, if you're curious about why I started this website, here's my thinking:

From now on, I will attempt to write my own blog posts because I'm a firm believer that **writing is thinking.** This is especially important now, when everything seems to be competing for our attention (social media) and we can be as productive as ever in a short amount of time (AI). As a natural consequence, our ability to think critically or go deep into our own thoughts is becoming increasingly difficult.

Writing is a solution to that. It’s how the great thinkers and builders of the pre-social media era, and of the AI era, pushed our society forward. Even though I already knew this notion, it has been an awakening for me since I reread Paul Graham’s post [Writes and Write-Nots](https://www.paulgraham.com/writes.html) just a few weeks ago. Deep, clear thinking is becoming rare these days, and I want to be one of the few who still has it.
